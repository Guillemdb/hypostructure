## Abstract

This document presents a machine-checkable Hypostructure proof object for neutral Tannakian duality.

The thin algebraic instance is built from a $k$-linear abelian rigid monoidal category $\mathcal{C}$ together with an exact faithful $k$-linear tensor functor
$$
\omega:\mathcal{C}\to \mathrm{Vect}_k^{\mathrm{fd}}.
$$
The finite-description permit is carried by the fiber functor, the stiffness permit is carried by the condition $\mathrm{End}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbb 1)=k$, and the Lock route uses the compiled Tannakian backend rather than generic tactic exhaustion. The bad pattern is a rigid tensor category of the same coarse type lacking any exact faithful tensor functor to finite-dimensional vector spaces. The invariant mismatch is immediate: any admissible morphism into $\mathcal{C}$ would compose with $\omega$ to produce the forbidden dictionary on the bad side.

The designated goal certificate is the reconstruction certificate
$$
K_{\mathrm{TannRec}}^+
=
\bigl(G,\ \mathcal{C}\simeq \mathrm{Rep}_k(G),\ \omega \leftrightarrow \text{forgetful functor}\bigr),
$$
with
$$
G=\underline{\mathrm{Aut}}^{\otimes}(\omega).
$$

## Theorem Statement

::::{prf:theorem} Tannakian Duality
:label: thm-tannakian-main

Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a $k$-linear abelian rigid monoidal category with
$$
\mathrm{End}_{\mathcal{C}}(\mathbb 1)=k,
$$
and let
$$
\omega:\mathcal{C}\to \mathrm{Vect}_k^{\mathrm{fd}}
$$
be an exact faithful $k$-linear tensor functor.

Then there exists an affine group scheme
$$
G=\underline{\mathrm{Aut}}^{\otimes}(\omega)
$$
over $k$ such that
$$
\mathcal{C}\simeq \mathrm{Rep}_k(G)
$$
as $k$-linear tensor categories, and under this equivalence the functor $\omega$ identifies with the forgetful functor.
