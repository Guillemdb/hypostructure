# S10: exterior-regular discard

This note proves the exterior-regular discard payload used in S4 and S8. The
certificate
\[
K_{\mathrm{ExtRegDiscard}}^+
\]
means that critical mass carried a positive physical distance away from the
Type II blowup point is assigned to the regular exterior ledger and has no
effect on compact terminal cameras after harmless pressure constants are
removed.

---

## S10.1 (Exterior regularity hypothesis)

Let \(u,p\) solve NS3D on \(\mathbb R^3\times[0,T^*)\), and let
\((x_*,T^*)\) be the only Type II core point under consideration. The
single-point exterior-regularity certificate
\[
K_{\mathrm{SinglePointBlowup}}^+
\]
means that for every \(r>0\), every \(R<\infty\), and every integer \(k\ge0\),
there are \(\delta>0\) and \(C_{r,R,k}<\infty\) such that
\[
\sup_{t\in(T^*-\delta,T^*)}
\left(
\|u(t)\|_{C^k(\overline{B_R}\setminus B_r(x_*))}
+
\|p(t)\|_{C^k(\overline{B_R}\setminus B_r(x_*))}
\right)
\le C_{r,R,k}.
\]
This is the standard meaning of having no singular point outside \(x_*\) near
time \(T^*\).

---

## S10.2 (Divergence-free core/exterior decomposition)

Fix \(0<r_0<R_0<\infty\). Let \(\chi\in C_c^\infty(B_{2r_0}(x_*))\) satisfy
\[
\chi\equiv1\quad\text{on }B_{r_0}(x_*).
\]
Let
\[
g:=\nabla\chi\cdot u.
\]
The support of \(g\) lies in the annulus
\[
A_{r_0}:=B_{2r_0}(x_*)\setminus B_{r_0}(x_*),
\]
where \(u\) is smooth by \(K_{\mathrm{SinglePointBlowup}}^+\). Since
\[
\int_{A_{r_0}}g\,dx
=
\int_{\mathbb R^3}\nabla\chi\cdot u\,dx
=
-\int_{\mathbb R^3}\chi\,\nabla\cdot u\,dx
=0,
\]
the Bogovskii operator on \(A_{r_0}\) gives a vector field
\[
w=\mathcal B_{A_{r_0}}g
\]
supported in \(A_{r_0}\), smooth up to \(T^*\), and satisfying
\[
\nabla\cdot w=g.
\]
Define
\[
u_{\mathrm{core}}:=\chi u-w,
\qquad
u_{\mathrm{ext}}:=u-u_{\mathrm{core}}.
\]
Then
\[
\nabla\cdot u_{\mathrm{core}}
=
\nabla\chi\cdot u+\chi\nabla\cdot u-\nabla\cdot w=0,
\]
and therefore
\[
\nabla\cdot u_{\mathrm{ext}}=0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
u_{\mathrm{core}}=u
\quad\text{on }B_{r_0}(x_*),
\]
and
\[
u_{\mathrm{ext}}=0
\quad\text{on }B_{r_0}(x_*).
\]
All derivatives of \(u_{\mathrm{ext}}\) are uniformly bounded on compact
exterior spacetime regions up to \(T^*\).

## S10.3 (Exterior fields vanish in compact core cameras)

Let \((x_n,\lambda_n)\) be a core camera with
\[
x_n\to x_*,
\qquad
\lambda_n\to0.
\]
For a compact camera ball \(B_R\), define
\[
V_{\mathrm{ext}}^n(y,\tau)
:=
\lambda_n
u_{\mathrm{ext}}(x_n+\lambda_n y,t_n+\lambda_n^2\tau).
\]

### Lemma S10.1

For every compact \(B_R\times I\),
\[
V_{\mathrm{ext}}^n\equiv0
\]
on \(B_R\times I\) for all sufficiently large \(n\). Consequently
\[
V_{\mathrm{ext}}^n\to0
\quad\text{in every local }L^q_\tau W^{k,q}_y(B_R)\text{ norm}.
\]

#### Proof

Since \(x_n\to x_*\) and \(\lambda_n\to0\), for fixed \(R\),
\[
x_n+\lambda_n B_R\subset B_{r_0}(x_*)
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\). On \(B_{r_0}(x_*)\),
\[
u_{\mathrm{ext}}=0.
\]
Therefore \(V_{\mathrm{ext}}^n=0\) on \(B_R\times I\) for all large \(n\).

\(\square\)

## S10.4 (Exterior pressure is harmless modulo constants)

Let \(p_{\mathrm{ext}}\) denote any pressure contribution generated by source
terms supported in the exterior region
\[
\{|x-x_*|\ge r_0\}.
\]
In the core camera set
\[
P_{\mathrm{ext}}^n(y,\tau)
:=
\lambda_n^2
p_{\mathrm{ext}}(x_n+\lambda_n y,t_n+\lambda_n^2\tau).
\]

### Lemma S10.2

For every compact \(B_R\times I\), there are constants
\[
c_{n,R}(\tau)
\]
such that
\[
P_{\mathrm{ext}}^n-c_{n,R}
\to0
\quad\text{in }L^\infty_\tau C^1_y(B_R),
\]
and hence
\[
\nabla_yP_{\mathrm{ext}}^n\to0
\quad\text{in }L^1_\tau H^{-1}_y(B_R).
\]

#### Proof

The exterior pressure source is a positive physical distance from
\[
x_n+\lambda_nB_R
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\). Therefore \(p_{\mathrm{ext}}\) is harmonic,
smooth, and uniformly \(C^2\)-bounded on the physical neighborhood swept out
by the compact camera cylinder. Take
\[
c_{n,R}(\tau):=
\lambda_n^2p_{\mathrm{ext}}(x_n,t_n+\lambda_n^2\tau).
\]
By the mean value theorem,
\[
\|P_{\mathrm{ext}}^n-c_{n,R}\|_{C^1_y(B_R)}
\le
C_R\lambda_n^3
\sup|\nabla_xp_{\mathrm{ext}}|
+
C_R\lambda_n^4
\sup|\nabla_x^2p_{\mathrm{ext}}|.
\]
The right-hand side tends to zero because the physical derivatives are
uniformly bounded and \(\lambda_n\to0\). This proves the asserted convergence.

\(\square\)

## S10.5 (Exterior-regular discard theorem)

### Lemma S10.3 (positive-distance profiles are exterior)

Let \((x_n,\lambda_n)\) be a core camera with \(x_n\to x_*\) and
\(\lambda_n\to0\). Let \((z_n,\mu_n)\) be a profile sequence satisfying
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}|z_n-x_*|\ge r_*>0.
\]
Then this profile is invisible in every compact core camera:
\[
\Lambda_{\lambda_n,x_n}^{-1}\Lambda_{\mu_n,z_n}\phi
\to0
\]
locally in \(L^3_y(B_R)\), and its pressure contribution vanishes modulo
constants in the topology of Lemma S10.2. Hence every such profile belongs to
the exterior-regular ledger, not to the Type II core ledger.

#### Proof

For fixed \(R<\infty\), the physical camera ball
\[
x_n+\lambda_nB_R
\]
lies in \(B_{r_*/4}(x_*)\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), while the profile
center \(z_n\) lies outside \(B_{r_*/2}(x_*)\). Thus the profile is a positive
physical distance from the compact core camera. The exterior velocity part is
covered by Lemma S10.1 after the core/exterior decomposition, and the exterior
pressure part is covered by Lemma S10.2. The conclusion follows.

\(\square\)

### Theorem S10.4

Assume
\[
K_{\mathrm{SinglePointBlowup}}^+
\]
and the exterior smoothness it entails. Then
\[
K_{\mathrm{ExtRegDiscard}}^+
\]
holds: every component of the solution carried a positive physical distance
from the blowup point is assigned to the exterior-regular ledger and is
invisible in compact Type II core cameras, including its pressure contribution
modulo constants.

#### Proof

Use the divergence-free core/exterior decomposition of S10.2. The exterior
component is identically zero in every compact core camera for large \(n\) by
Lemma S10.1. Its pressure contribution is locally a scaled smooth harmonic
field and vanishes modulo constants by Lemma S10.2. Cutoff and Bogovskii
correction terms are supported in the fixed exterior annulus \(A_{r_0}\), so
they are also invisible in compact core cameras by the same argument.

Thus exterior regular mass can be removed from the Type II core ledger without
changing the local repaired-gauge equation in any compact terminal camera,
except by errors converging to zero in the S7/S8 perturbative topology. This
is exactly \(K_{\mathrm{ExtRegDiscard}}^+\).

\(\square\)
