\title[Local Caccioppoli and Rough Cores]{Local Caccioppoli Estimates and Rough-Core Exclusion}
\author{}
\date{}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We prove the local Caccioppoli estimate needed in the local CKN Type II analysis.
The assumptions are physical suitability, a local repaired chart, local pressure
decomposition, compact-cylinder CKN bounds, and bounded local modulation
coefficients.  The estimate controls the local suitable package on smaller cylinders
and gives windowed \(H^1\) control on bounded cores.  Consequently a rough core
is not an independent Type II assumption: it either satisfies the local
Caccioppoli bounds or enters the cascade/multibubble decomposition.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Renormalized local energy inequality}

Let \((V,P,a,b)\) solve the locally repaired renormalized equation on compact
cylinders.  The local energy inequality is the physical local energy inequality
pulled back by the absolutely continuous local chart.  It contains the usual
Navier--Stokes terms and lower-order terms involving \(a\) and \(b\).

\section{Caccioppoli estimate}

\begin{theorem}[Compact-cylinder Caccioppoli estimate]\label{thm:caccioppoli}
Let \(Q_{2R,I}=B_{2R}\times I\) be compactly contained in the local chart, and
let \(I'\Subset I\).  Suppose \((V,P)\) satisfies the renormalized local energy
inequality on \(Q_{2R,I}\),
\[
\int_I\int_{B_{2R}}
\left(|V|^3+|P-(P)_{B_{2R}}(\tau)|^{3/2}\right)<\infty,
\]
and \(a,b\) are bounded on \(I\).  Then
\[
\int_{I'}\int_{B_R}|\nabla V|^2
\le C\left(1+
\int_I\int_{B_{2R}}|V|^3+
\int_I\int_{B_{2R}}|P-(P)_{B_{2R}}|^{3/2}\right),
\]
where \(C\) depends on \(R\), \(I'\Subset I\), the viscosity, the cutoff
choice, and \(\|a\|_{L^\infty(I)}+\|b\|_{L^\infty(I)}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Insert a cutoff \(\phi(y,\tau)=\eta(\tau)\zeta(y)^2\) in the local energy
inequality, with \(\zeta=1\) on \(B_R\) and \(\operatorname{supp}\zeta\subset
B_{2R}\).  The convection and cutoff terms are bounded by the displayed local
\(L^3\) integral and cutoff constants.  The pressure term is estimated after
subtracting the spatial mean on \(B_{2R}\).  The lower-order modulation terms are bounded by
\(\|a\|_{L^\infty(I)}+\|b\|_{L^\infty(I)}\), the cutoff constants, and the
local \(L^2\) mass.  The local \(L^2\) mass is controlled on compact balls by
the local \(L^3\) bound.
The interior gradient term is absorbed into the left-hand side after the usual
Caccioppoli rearrangement.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Rough-core alternative]\label{cor:rough-alternative}
On unit windows where the local modulation coefficients are uniformly bounded,
\cref{thm:caccioppoli} gives uniform local windowed \(H^1\) control on every
bounded core.  If the compact-cylinder suitable package fails instead, then the
local Caccioppoli estimate implies failure of the local \(C+D\) bound on a
comparable cylinder.  Thus rough-core behavior belongs to the local single-core
criterion or to the local multibubble/cascade analysis.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The first statement is \cref{thm:caccioppoli} applied on consecutive windows.
For the second, argue by contrapositive: if the local \(C+D\) quantities and the
modulation coefficients are bounded on a slightly larger cylinder, then
\cref{thm:caccioppoli} bounds \(E\) on a smaller cylinder; the compact-ball
\(L^3\) bound also controls the local \(L^2\) mass, hence \(A\).  Therefore
failure of the suitable package must come from local CKN concentration on a
comparable cylinder.
\end{proof}
